Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions

How does RIP-7212 enable P-256 Passkeys on EVM? +
RIP-7212 specifies a native precompile at address 0x100 for P-256 (secp256r1) signature verification. Instead of spending 350,000+ gas on Solidity curve emulation, the precompile executes in native C++ on Base Sepolia for under 10,000 gas.
What happens if an attacker drains with thousands of small $9 transfers? +
The smart account enforces a cumulative 24-hour rolling window spend cap in addition to per-operation thresholds. Once the rolling spend meets the daily cap, the wallet automatically locks down all 1-signature transfers and requires the 2-of-2 hardware quorum.
What happens if I lose my MacBook or primary device? +
Your account is configured with a multi-key quorum. Signer #1 (e.g. your hardware YubiKey or secondary phone) combined with your social recovery or backup key can execute a self-call to replace Signer #0 with a new hardware device.
Is the Chrome Extension compatible with Uniswap, Aave, and OpenSea? +
Yes. The extension implements both EIP-1193 (window.ethereum) and EIP-6963 provider announcement. Any dApp can connect, query balances, and request transactions which are then evaluated by our on-chain firewall.
