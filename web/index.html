Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work without a seed phrase?

Haptix uses WebAuthn and your device's Secure Enclave (like FaceID or TouchID) to generate a secure P-256 private key that never leaves your device.

Are there any gas fees?

No. All transactions are sponsored transparently via an ERC-4337 Paymaster natively integrated into the Haptix smart account.

Can I use Haptix on my phone and laptop?

Yes. Passkeys natively sync across your ecosystem using iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager, allowing you to sign from any device.

What happens if I lose my device?

Because passkeys sync to your cloud account, simply logging into a new device with your iCloud or Google account will instantly restore access to your wallet.
