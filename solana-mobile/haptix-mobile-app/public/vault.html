How dual-device signing works

  • Your phone requests, it can't approve. Transfers over 0.1 SOL automatically require this second device to sign off.
  • This Mac holds the secondary key. Touch ID unlocks an Apple Secure Enclave key that physically never leaves the chip.
  • Approval travels out-of-band. Both signatures are combined on Solana using the native Secp256r1 precompile.
