# Welcome to Haptix

Haptix is a non-custodial smart wallet designed for a world where humans and AI agents manage assets together. 

Instead of relying on a fragile 12-word seed phrase, Haptix secures your assets using **WebAuthn Passkeys** backed by your device's hardware enclave (e.g., Apple Secure Enclave, Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello). 

## The Core Problem

Traditional wallets are designed for manual human signing. If you want an AI agent to execute a trade, you are forced to hand over your private key. If the agent makes a mistake or gets hacked, your entire wallet is drained.

## The Haptix Solution

Haptix uses **Account Abstraction (ERC-4337)** to enforce spending policies directly on the blockchain. 
You can generate a scoped **Session Key** for an AI agent, granting it permission to trade autonomously, but only under strict, on-chain constraints.

- **No Seed Phrases:** Secure your wallet with your biometrics.
- **AI Agent Delegation:** Let AI bots trade for you, safely.
- **3-Tier On-Chain Firewall:** Limits are enforced by the blockchain, not just the UI.
- **Guardian Social Recovery:** Recover your wallet even if you lose your phone.
