# Getting Started

Setting up Haptix is designed to be frictionless. There are no seed phrases to copy down or store. 

## 1. Install the App

Haptix is available as a browser extension and a mobile application.
- **Browser Extension:** Install from the Chrome Web Store or load unpacked from the `/extension` directory.
- **Mobile App:** Download the iOS or Android app (currently in development on Solana Devnet).

## 2. Create Your Smart Account

When you first open Haptix, select **Create New Wallet**.

### Passkey Enrollment

Haptix requires you to set up two passkeys to establish a secure **2-of-2 Hardware Quorum**:
1. **Primary Passkey (Device Auth):** Typically tied to your current device (e.g., your phone's Secure Enclave).
2. **Secondary Passkey (Backup Auth):** This acts as your backup and quorum key. 

Follow the biometric prompts (Face ID, Touch ID, etc.) to generate these keys securely on your hardware.

## 3. Fund Your Wallet

Your Smart Account address is a deterministic PDA (Program Derived Address) or CREATE2 contract address. 
You can send assets (ETH, SOL, USDC) to this address from any exchange or other wallet.
