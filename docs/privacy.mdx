# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Overview

Haptix is a non-custodial Web3 smart contract wallet for Solana Mobile and browser clients powered by hardware-bound WebAuthn passkeys (NIST P-256). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**Haptix collects ZERO personal data, analytics, or telemetry.**

- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device locations.
- **No Keystroke or Activity Tracking:** We do not track your browsing history or your interactions on decentralized apps.
- **No Private Keys or Biometric Data:** Passkey private keys are generated and stored exclusively within your device's physical hardware enclave (Apple Secure Enclave, Android Keystore / Titan M2, Windows Hello, YubiKey). Biometric data (fingerprint or facial scan) **never leaves your device** and is never accessible to Haptix, smart contracts, or any backend server.

---

## 3. Local Storage Usage

The Haptix client stores strictly operational preferences locally on your physical device:

- Enrolled public keys (P-256 raw 64-byte public key coordinates $x, y$)
- Configured smart account addresses (PDAs)
- Network preferences (e.g., Solana Devnet, Mainnet)
- Session token thresholds and spending policy configurations
- Cached transaction receipts for your personal inspection

This data remains stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any centralized server.

---

## 4. Network Requests & Third Parties

When you initiate an on-chain operation or view account balances, the client communicates directly with:

- **Public Blockchain RPC Providers:** To read account balances and broadcast signed transactions directly to Solana validators or public paymaster nodes.
- **Public Decentralized Oracles / Price APIs:** To fetch token exchange rates (e.g., Pyth Network, Jupiter Price API).

These requests do not contain any personally identifiable information.

---

## 5. Security of Your Data

Haptix is non-custodial. All operations are governed directly by open-source Anchor smart contracts deployed on the Solana blockchain. You maintain complete, mathematical ownership over your account.
