How dual-device signing works

Physical security enforced by two separate cryptographic enclaves.

Step 1 Mobile Phone Initiates Transfer

Transfers above limit escalate to dual-device mode. Your phone signs first, but cannot broadcast without second device co-signature.

Step 2 Biometrics Unlock Hardware Key

Your second device receives the out-of-band request. Biometric authentication authorizes an isolated P-256 private key that never leaves the hardware secure chip.

Step 3 Native Solana Precompile Verification

Both hardware signatures are verified atomically on-chain via Solana’s native Secp256r1 precompile without relying on centralized relays.
