Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work without a seed phrase?

Haptix uses WebAuthn and your device's hardware enclave (Android StrongBox or Apple Secure Enclave) to generate a secure P-256 (Secp256r1) keypair that never leaves your device. Solana natively verifies these signatures on-chain via the Secp256r1SigVerify precompile.

How does 2-of-2 Hardware Quorum protect funds?

You define a single-key spend ceiling (e.g. 0.10 SOL). Any transaction exceeding that threshold requires two distinct hardware passkey signatures (your phone + secondary device running the Haptix Web Vault at /vault.html).

Are there any gas fees on onboarding?

No. New users onboard with zero SOL in their pocket. Our gasless paymaster sponsors smart account PDA initialization and drips starter test SOL automatically.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Because passkeys sync to your Google or Apple cloud keystore, signing in on your new device restores your exact on-chain smart wallet PDA. In emergencies, your designated recovery guardians can trigger an on-chain Panic Freeze to stop outflows.
