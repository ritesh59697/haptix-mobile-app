How Social Recovery Works

Non-custodial security for lost or stolen phones on Solana.

Step 1
Guardian Freezes Lost Device

The designated guardian calls the on-chain Anchor program to freeze the wallet and propose replacement passkeys.

Step 2
24-Hour Dispute Window

A timelock prevents rogue guardians from stealing funds. The real wallet owner can cancel the recovery anytime if the device is not lost.

Step 3
Seamless Device Restoration

Once the dispute window expires, the replacement phone enrolls its biometric passkey and takes over full wallet control.
