How dual-device signing works

  • Your phone requests, it can't approve. Transfers over 0.1 SOL need a second device to sign off.
  • This Mac holds the other key. Touch ID unlocks a Secure Enclave key that never leaves the chip.
  • Approval travels out-of-band. The two devices never share a network path, so one compromised device isn't enough.
