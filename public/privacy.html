Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026
Executive Summary: Haptix is architected with strict zero-knowledge, non-custodial privacy. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal identity, biometric samples, seed phrases, or private cryptographic keys.

1. Privacy Architecture & Non-Custodial Design

Haptix operates as a direct client interface to the Solana blockchain. We do not maintain centralized user databases, accounts requiring username/passwords, or server-side key custody.

2. Biometric Data & Hardware Passkeys

Haptix utilizes WebAuthn and FIDO2 standards (`secp256r1`) directly backed by your mobile or desktop hardware security chips (such as Android StrongBox Keymaster, Qualcomm TEE, or Apple Secure Enclave).

  • Local Biometric Processing: Your biometric sensor data (fingerprints, facial recognition) is processed exclusively on-device by your operating system’s secure hardware. It is never exposed to Haptix or transmitted across any network.
  • Public Key Derivation: Only the resulting public key coordinate and non-reversible ECDSA signatures are signed and sent to the Solana blockchain for smart account validation.

3. Information Collected (Or Not Collected)

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do NOT collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, or government identification.
  • Private Keys & Seeds: Haptix eliminates seed phrases entirely. We never possess, view, or store any private signing credentials.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do NOT use invasive advertising tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting, or selling of usage data to third-party data brokers.

4. Public Blockchain Records

When you initiate a transaction or register a smart account via Haptix, your account address, transaction parameters, and cryptographic signatures are broadcast to the decentralized Solana network.

Public blockchain ledgers are transparent and permanent by design. Please be aware that your on-chain activity is visible to validators, block explorers, and the public.

5. Network Requests & RPC Nodes

To interact with the Solana blockchain, the application communicates with Solana RPC endpoints (such as public or dedicated Solana cluster endpoints). RPC nodes process incoming RPC requests (e.g. `getBalance`, `sendTransaction`) in accordance with their standard network routing. Users may configure custom RPC providers if desired.

6. Security of Your Devices

Your assets are secured by the hardware protection on your physical device. We strongly recommend configuring biometric locks, enabling OS device encryption, and linking a secondary hardware signer (e.g., dual-device quorum) for maximum asset protection.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
